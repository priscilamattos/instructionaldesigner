I bring an Instructional Designer’s structure and a UX designer’s attention to the learner.

My background includes developing and maintaining technical, compliance, and sales training in regulated environments. I work with subject matter experts to make complex processes clearer, create purposeful practice, and keep learning content accurate as needs change.

I’ve worked in financial services and workforce development, with experience at Bank of Montreal, Corporate Courseware, and ISANS. From intake through publishing, I balance learner needs with project milestones and stakeholder feedback.
